Research Experience
Graduate Research Associate, The Ohio State University
Aug 2023 – present
  • Probabilistic world models from partial, noisy observations (NeurIPS 2026, first author)
    • Developed K-PWM, a probabilistic world model pairing Koopman-bilinear latent dynamics with a neural (MLP) observation decoder; latent states are inferred through the learned model itself (EKF/EKS), with no separate inference network.
    • Cut long-horizon prediction error 38–66% against the strongest of five baselines (MTS3) on partially observed MuJoCo tasks, learning from 15 trajectories per task and training 7.6–15.1× faster.
    • Closed the loop with uncertainty-aware MPPI planning on four simulated manipulation platforms (Franka Panda, TIAGo++, ALOHA 2, Allegro Hand): 0.8–6.1 mm end-effector tracking RMSE at 5.6–22 ms per step, 4.3–17× faster than MTS3.
  • Learned world model and real-time planning for quadrotor flight (under review)
    • Learned ABS-PWM, a probabilistic quadrotor world model, from 90 s of noisy simulated flight data using EM with latent-state smoothing; 100-step rollouts in 1.3 ms, 58–64× faster than KNODE, with 63% lower out-of-distribution attitude error.
    • Built BUC-MPC, a convex uncertainty-aware planner: 5.24 ms per control step on a Skydio X2 in MuJoCo, 1.4–57× faster than KoopNet, SkyJEPA, KNODE and FNN controllers, with the highest flight completion under dynamics shifts (86%).
  • Real-robot deployment: quadrotor and wheeled robot (IEEE RA-L + ICRA 2026; RA-L under review)
    • Flew a physical quadrotor (NVIDIA Jetson NX, PX4) on four flight tasks with tracking RMSE within 3.7 cm of nonlinear MPC, at 1.9–4.3× lower solve time in simulation. Open-source code with 100 GitHub stars.
    • Built KOBRA-MPC for wheeled robots, validated on two simulated platforms and a physical TurtleBot3 (2–7 cm tracking RMSE, 4.1–4.8 ms mean computation time), and integrated a higher-order control barrier function for obstacle avoidance.
  • GPU-accelerated optimization for real-time control (FAME-QP, open source)
    • Developed FAME-QP, a GPU QP solver (PyTorch, Triton, CUDA graphs): 1.5–4.3× faster than the best GPU baseline (ReLU-QP) and up to 25.9× faster than the fastest CPU solver (DAQP) on dense QPs with 220–1,400 variables.
    • Cut QP latency 2.3–3.1× in 500 Hz Unitree G1 humanoid push recovery (MuJoCo), doubling the horizon that fits the 2 ms budget.
