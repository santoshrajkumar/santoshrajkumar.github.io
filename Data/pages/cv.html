Research Experience
The Ohio State University, Columbus, OH
Aug 2023 – Present
Graduate Research Associate
  • GPU-Accelerated Quadratic Programming (QP) for Real-Time Predictive Control
    • Developed FAME-QP (Fused ADMM with Multimode Execution), a GPU solver for the dense QPs of linear MPC, combining constraint-dependent operator representations with fused single- and two-kernel iterations replayed through CUDA graphs; the operator representations cut storage by up to 4× and iteration time by up to 3×.
    • On random dense QPs, outperformed all evaluated GPU solvers at every size and all CPU solvers for n ≥ 220: 1.5–4.3× faster than ReLU-QP and 1.2–25.9× faster than DAQP over n = 220–1,400, at matched accuracy.
    • On random linear MPC, achieved the lowest mean step time at every size (0.66–4.23 ms), 3.2–7.3× faster than ReLU-QP and 3.2–13.8× faster than πMPC.
    • On Unitree G1 humanoid push recovery (MuJoCo), reduced mean QP time by 2.3–3.1× versus ReLU-QP, staying within the 2 ms control period at every horizon and doubling the largest feasible horizon (N = 10 to 20).
    • On Skydio X2 quadrotor tracking with QP matrices updated every step, achieved 12.5 ms mean QP time versus 59.2 ms for CuPIQP (4.7×), with 17.6 ms 99th-percentile latency within the 20 ms control period and 2.7 mm position RMSE.
  • Data-efficient Structured World Models for Real-Time Robot Planning and Control (NeurIPS 2026)
    • Developed K-PWM, a probabilistic world model coupling Koopman-bilinear latent dynamics with a nonlinear decoder and extended Kalman filtering and smoothing, learning from noisy, partial observations without a separately trained inference network.
    • Designed a generalized expectation conditional maximization (GECM) training procedure with closed-form updates for the generators, noise covariances, and latent prior, plus a Koopman-consistent initialization that selects the latent dimension from data.
    • On partially observed Gymnasium MuJoCo systems (Hopper, Swimmer, Half Cheetah), reduced mean prediction RMSE by 38–66% against the most accurate baseline (MTS3) with 7.6–15.1× faster training.
    • Integrated uncertainty-aware MPPI control on four MuJoCo Menagerie robots (Franka Panda, TIAGo++, ALOHA 2, Allegro Hand), achieving 0.8–6.1 mm end-effector tracking RMSE, among the two most accurate methods on every robot, with 5.6–22.0 ms per-step computation, 4.3–17.0× faster than MTS3.
  • Structured Bilinear World Model and Convex MPC for Quadrotor Control (ICLR 2027 submission)
    • Developed a world model combining novel analytical bilinear dynamics with probabilistic learning to identify vehicle parameters and noise statistics from a few minutes of noisy simulated flight data.
    • Designed an EM-based training method with latent-state smoothing and closed-form covariance updates; achieved 100-step rollouts in 1.3 ms, 1.4–64× faster than the baselines.
    • Built BUC-MPC, exploiting shared model structure for convex trajectory optimization and uncertainty-weighted disturbance compensation; achieved 5.24 ms mean control latency, 1.4–57× faster than the baselines.
    • Validated 100 Hz control on Skydio X2 (MuJoCo), achieving 0.18 m nominal tracking RMSE, 100% nominal flight completion, and 86% completion under vehicle and environmental shifts.
  • Learning Linear Models of Time-Delay Systems with Provable Error Guarantees (IEEE CDC 2026)
    • Developed a data-driven Koopman framework that converts infinite-dimensional nonlinear delay dynamics into finite-dimensional linear models for prediction.
    • Built an end-to-end pipeline combining history sampling, kernel-based extended DMD, and physical-state reconstruction, with deterministic error bounds separating discretization, interpolation, and regression contributions.
    • Validated prediction on nonlinear feedback and tumor–immune systems using 100 held-out trajectories per system, demonstrating accuracy gains with finer history resolution and denser data.
  • Real-Time Quadrotor Control with Analytical Koopman Models (IEEE RA-L + ICRA 2026)
    • Developed a novel analytical Koopman representation of quadrotor dynamics on SE(3) to enable predictive control without training data while preserving the four-dimensional control input.
    • Formulated trajectory tracking as a convex quadratic program with state and input constraints; established controllability and practical closed-loop stability under stated assumptions.
    • Achieved 0.32–1.31 ms mean solver time, 1.9–4.3× faster than nonlinear MPC, across four simulated tracking tasks and four prediction horizons.
    • Validated on a physical quadrotor equipped with NVIDIA Jetson NX and PX4, achieving 15.6–29.8 cm tracking RMSE across four flight tasks, within 3.7 cm of nonlinear MPC; released code and demonstrations.
  • Wheeled Robot Bilinearization, Control, & Safety (IEEE RA-L & ACC 2027 submissions, IROS 2026 LBR)
    • Developed a novel analytical bilinear Koopman form preserving SE(2) geometry and nonholonomic dynamics, with closed-form state reconstruction and provably vanishing truncation-error bounds.
    • Built KOBRA-MPC, converting lifted dynamics into constrained convex QPs; validated on two simulated platforms and a physical TurtleBot3, achieving 2–7 cm tracking RMSE and 4.1–4.8 ms mean computation time in indoor and outdoor experiments.
    • Designed lifted-space LQR with physical-input recovery, enforcing actuator and velocity limits through a strongly convex QP; established optimizer uniqueness, finite-horizon feasibility, and practical tracking stability.
    • Integrated a higher-order control barrier function for obstacle avoidance within the same QP, evaluating safety directly on physical dynamics without truncation error.
